01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE HANDLE

Security is my work. Curiosity connects it all.

I'm Osama, known online as Osaa601. I work in information security in Libya's financial-services sector, with a background in networks and IT infrastructure.

Since 2022, my work has included security monitoring, incident investigation, awareness, infrastructure reviews, and security documentation. I enjoy turning a technical problem into a clear plan that people can act on.

✦

Away from the desk: anime, Tolkien's worlds, retro games, lo-fi music, hiking, and taekwondo. Those interests shape my creative work—and this little corner of the internet.

How I work

I start by understanding the problem and the environment, then investigate, explain options, and document the outcome. I aim to leave people with a clear next step and a useful handover.

Across security and infrastructure

My experience connects monitoring and investigation with networks, Linux/Windows administration, access management, support, and security governance. I work with technical teams and communicate findings to the people making decisions.

The creative side

I have edited videos since around age ten. Content creation, filmmaking, gaming, and Unity/Unreal/VR experiments give me another way to explore ideas. My cooperative game prototype is an ongoing personal project.

Beyond the screen

I am based in Tripoli and work in Arabic and English. Anime, Tolkien, retro games, lo-fi and heavier music, hiking, basketball, and taekwondo all find their way into my interests. I hold a second-dan taekwondo black belt.

FROM FIRST MESSAGE TO HANDOVER

A clear way to work remotely.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Understand the problem, context, and desired outcome.

  2. 02

    Scope

    Agree the boundaries, access, and responsibilities.

  3. 03

    Proposal

    Confirm deliverables, estimate, review points, and terms.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Work through the scope with agreed progress updates.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Explain the findings, leave usable material, and agree next steps.

A defined project

A bounded review, documentation phase, infrastructure task, or editing brief with agreed outputs.

Limited monthly advisory

An agreed number of review sessions, advisory hours, or content deliverables. Capacity and response expectations are confirmed in the proposal; this is not a 24/7 response service.

Timing is an estimate, subject to scope, access, feedback, and scheduling. Fees are proposed after discovery. Outcomes and certification are not guaranteed.

Discuss an engagement

We agree on scope, access, deliverables, and timing before work starts. Security testing requires authorization; compliance services support readiness rather than issuing certification.
