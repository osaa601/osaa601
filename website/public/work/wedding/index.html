Don't Ruin the Wedding!

Unreal EngineCo-opMultiplayer

In development · prototype

Overview

Don't Ruin the Wedding! is my independent cooperative-game project. It brings together game creation, iteration, and the technical challenges of getting several players to share the same experience.

Areas of work

  • Cooperative gameplay and iteration in Unreal Engine.
  • Local split-screen play.
  • Listen-server multiplayer experimentation.
  • Replicated gameplay systems and testing.
Current stage

The game is in development. Local split-screen, listen-server play, and replication have been tested in the prototype. It is not presented as a released product.
